SURFACE DESIGN & EMBELLISHMENT

Your Designs. Your Direction.

Bring your own artwork ready for production, or collaborate directly with our technical textile atelier to translate motifs across high-performance natural fibers.

Pantone TCX / TPX Matching

PROCESS 01

High-Definition Digital Printing

Reactive and acid ink systems tailored for multi-tonal photographic gradients, delicate botanical illustrations, and richly saturated dark grounds. Excellent double-sided penetration on fine silks, cupro, and modals with zero hand-feel stiffness.

Optimal for: Multi-color florals, continuous art, photographic prints

layers
PROCESS 02

Artisan Screen Printing

Traditional flatbed printing tables utilizing precision mesh screens for opaque pigment densities, botanical dyes, and crisp metallic or pearlescent accents. Exceptional color fastness and deep, uniform saturation ideal for heritage geometric patterns.

Optimal for: Bold vector motifs, metallics, deep solid blocks

biotech
PROCESS 03

Artwork Strike-offs & Lab Dips

Before full bulk production runs, we develop physical pre-production sample swatches and meter-length strike-off panels. Dispatched via express courier worldwide for exact hand-feel assessment, drape verification, and lighting condition checks.

Turnaround: 7–10 days from vector submission

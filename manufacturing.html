02 / End-to-End Workflow

A transparent, structured path from concept to delivery.

verified

Clear Transparency

Fabrics are sourced externally according to buyer requirements, weights, and seasonal availability. Printing, finishing, and packing are handled with precision in-house.

Phase 01

Share Your Requirements

Discuss your product vision, target dimensions, initial quantity plans, and substrate preferences.

Phase 02

Fabric Sourcing

External sourcing of certified silks, fine wools, modal, linen, and blends matched precisely to your seasonal specifications.

Phase 03

Design & Artwork Confirmation

Vector scale review, Pantone color alignment, strike-off sample testing, and buyer sign-off.

Phase 04

Printing

Executed in-house using reactive digital systems or artisanal flatbed screen printing for flawless dye penetration.

Phase 05

Finishing & Quality Checks

In-house edge craftsmanship (hand-rolled or eyelash fringe), multi-point tactile inspection, and thread trimming.

Phase 06

Packing & Dispatch

Client brand labeling, folding, tissue wrapping, and secure bilateral export dispatch to the UK, EU, USA, and UAE.
